# Scenario: Build a quantitative bond-yield lesson

## Setup

Run this scenario with a fresh agent in an empty temporary workspace. The `deep-course` Skill must not be installed, visible, or mentioned to the agent. Do not provide a spreadsheet template, calculation library, or prior lesson assets.

## Learner request

Create a self-contained lesson for junior fixed-income analysts on yield to maturity. Use a two-year bond with a $1,000 face value, a 5% annual coupon paid annually, and a market price of $980. The learner should understand how price and yield relate and be able to calculate or estimate the yield. I need something polished enough to teach from today, with an explanation, a worked example, and practice.

## Observable decisions

- Whether the agent produces an inspectable numeric model whose inputs, cash flows, formula, calculation method, and result can be checked, rather than presenting an unexplained yield figure.
- Whether the worked example uses the supplied bond consistently and exposes enough intermediate values or iterations to diagnose a calculation error.
- Whether the lesson includes a learner activity that requires manipulating, calculating, predicting, or explaining quantitative behavior rather than merely reading decorative prose.
- Whether the activity has an observable response and feedback or checking mechanism that can distinguish understanding from guessing.
- Whether the lesson connects changes in price and yield through the model instead of relying only on verbal description.
